# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - Unreleased

First release.

- One-click collapse and expand of the menu bar icons to the left of a single button
- 19 icon styles plus custom text, with adjustable size and weight, and button opacity
- Global keyboard shortcut (default ⌃⌥S), reveal on hover, and a right-click Hide/Show item
- Auto-hide with a slider delay, optionally only on battery or mains power
- Hides when the Mac locks or sleeps, and when a display is mirrored
- Launch at login, and settings export and import
- Update check (once a day, can be turned off) that links to a newer release and never downloads anything
- Translated into 36 languages (British English is the default)
- Requires macOS 27 on Apple silicon
